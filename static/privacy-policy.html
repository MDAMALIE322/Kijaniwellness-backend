Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Kijani Wellness ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Damalie Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Kijani Wellness mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Account information: Email address and password (hashed)
  • Body profile: Height, weight, age, biological sex (all optional)
  • Food logs: Meals you scan or log manually
  • Workout sessions: Exercises, sets, reps, duration
  • Fasting logs: Fasting windows and notes
  • Herbal logs: Herbs you log for personal tracking
  • Voice notes: Processed for transcription only — audio is not stored
  • Food photos: Used for AI meal analysis only
  • Subscription status: Plan and billing information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized AI wellness coaching and progress tracking
  • To generate meal plans, workout suggestions, and weekly summaries
  • To improve app features and user experience
  • To send important account and service notifications

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your health or fitness data
  • We do not use your health data for advertising
  • We do not share your personal data with third-party advertisers
  • We do not use your data for data mining or profiling for commercial purposes

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers. All data is transmitted using TLS encryption. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Groq AI: For food image analysis and voice transcription (data processed but not stored by Groq)
  • Railway: For secure server hosting
  • Apple / Google: For authentication and payment processing

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all your data
  • Export: Download your data in JSON format from Settings → Privacy
  • Delete: Permanently delete your account and all data from Settings → Privacy → Delete Account
  • Correct: Update your profile information at any time

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Kijani Wellness is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Health Data Disclaimer

Kijani Wellness provides general fitness and wellness information only. It is not medical advice. We do not collect or process clinical health data. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making major diet, exercise, fasting, or supplement changes.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email.
